Who We Are

More Than a Group

5ive is a brotherhood built on the belief that your character defines you — not your bank account, your background, or your beliefs. We are five individuals from different walks of life who chose to come together and grow as one.

We are driven by a commitment to health, both physically and mentally. A healthy body cultivates a healthy mind, and together we push each other to be better every single day.

We don't see color. We don't see faith. We don't see ethnicity. We see potential, heart, and the willingness to show up.
